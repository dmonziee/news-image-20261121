Also on the front page: a 3.7% AI stock rout dragging US markets into correction, the World Court ruling Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya, a trial cancer pill making pancreatic tumours undetectable in 1 in 12 patients, Gen Z protests sweeping 25 European countries, Britain and the EU signing their biggest post-Brexit deals, and COP31 climate talks in Antalya running overtime with no fossil-fuel agreement.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to the output directory. The image was drawn by the agent with PIL from the day's reported news — no outside images were used.
